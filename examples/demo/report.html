Definitions and limits

CTR = clicks / impressions; CPC = spend / clicks; CVR = attributed orders / clicks; ROAS = attributed sales / spend; ACOS = spend / attributed sales; TACOS = ad spend / same-period store sales. Ratios are calculated after summing their numerators and denominators. A zero denominator displays “—”.

Estimated attributed contribution = attributed sales × (1 − SKU variable cost rate) − ad spend. It is shown only when all advertised SKUs have a cost rate. It is neither audited net profit nor proof of incremental ad impact. Review inventory, competition, price, and attribution lag before acting.
