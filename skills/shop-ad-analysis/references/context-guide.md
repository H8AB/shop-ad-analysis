# Platform, market, category, and company context

The deterministic engine calculates comparable metrics; the agent must apply supplied business context before finalizing conclusions. Do not claim a platform, site, or category benchmark unless a traceable current source or user-provided target exists.

## Platform

Record ad product, report grain, attribution event/window, sales definition, placement and bidding controls, and whether search terms are available. Route a recommendation only to controls supported by that platform. A missing keyword/search-term export means no search-term diagnosis.

## Site

Check currency, time zone, taxes, shipping geography, local campaign calendar, and whether the periods contain promotions or holidays. Compare separate sites independently unless FX and attribution have been explicitly harmonized.

## Category and SKU

Ask whether purchase cycles, repeat purchases, price bands, return rates, and inventory constraints materially affect the decision. Interpret high clicks with low orders as a diagnostic signal; inspect listing, availability, pricing, and traffic intent before changing bids. A category name alone is not a benchmark.

## Enterprise

Use the company's variable cost policy, objective, target ROAS/ACOS, cash-flow and inventory limits. If `business_context.notes` conflicts with a generic rule (for example, a strong SKU has uncertain replenishment), qualify or withhold a budget-growth suggestion. If an agent modifies the HTML narrative, retain the engine-calculated figures and data-quality warnings.
